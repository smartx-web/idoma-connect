ANNUAL CULTURAL FESTIVAL

Eje-Alekwu Festival

Honouring ancestral heritage, cultural identity and the enduring traditions of the Idoma people.

OVERVIEW

A Celebration of Ancestral Heritage

Eje-Alekwu is an annual cultural celebration associated with the ancestral heritage of the Idoma people. It provides an important occasion for communities to reconnect with their cultural roots and strengthen communal bonds.

The celebration brings together members of the community through traditional activities, music, cultural performances and communal gatherings, helping to preserve indigenous knowledge and traditions for younger generations.

CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE

Why the Festival Matters

Eje-Alekwu provides an important space for cultural continuity. Through the participation of elders, families and younger generations, traditions and knowledge are passed from one generation to another.
