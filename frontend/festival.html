ANNUAL CULTURAL FESTIVAL

Igede-Agba Festival

Celebrating heritage, harvest and the enduring identity of the Igede people.

OVERVIEW

A Celebration of Identity

Igede-Agba is one of the most celebrated annual festivals within the Igede-speaking communities of Benue State. It brings together sons, daughters and visitors in a colourful display of culture, unity and gratitude.

The festival is widely associated with the harvest season and serves as an occasion for thanksgiving, community reunion and the preservation of indigenous traditions through music, dance and traditional ceremonies.

CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE

Why the Festival Matters

Beyond celebration, Igede-Agba reinforces community identity by preserving language, traditional attire, royal customs, oral history and intergenerational knowledge. Elders, youth and cultural groups all participate in keeping the heritage alive.
