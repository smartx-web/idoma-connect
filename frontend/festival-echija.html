ANNUAL CULTURAL FESTIVAL

Echija Festival

Celebrating culture, community and the enduring heritage of the Idoma people.

OVERVIEW

A Celebration of Cultural Heritage

Echija is an annual cultural celebration that brings communities together to celebrate their heritage, strengthen communal relationships and preserve indigenous traditions.

The festival provides an opportunity for people of different generations to reconnect with their cultural identity through traditional activities, music, dance, storytelling and communal celebration.

CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE

Keeping the Heritage Alive

Cultural festivals such as Echija provide an important connection between the past and the present. They create spaces where elders can share knowledge while younger generations experience and participate in their heritage.

Through continued celebration, documentation and participation, important aspects of indigenous culture can remain visible and relevant to future generations.
